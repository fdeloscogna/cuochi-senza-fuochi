Who we are

Since March 2022

The dessert is easy. The group is the recipe.

Cuochi senza fuochi started in Padova in March 2022, among people who lived a few streets apart, had never spoken to each other, and between them owned about one working oven. We still don’t use ovens. We do have each other’s numbers now.

our mission

Help people meet, over something sweet and cheap.

We bring strangers around one table to make an easy dessert together.

Making something with your hands takes the pressure off. You don’t have to think of what to say, because there is always a biscuit to crush or a bowl to pass. By the time the cake comes out of the fridge, you’re not strangers any more.

We keep it cheap, so nobody stays home because of money. We keep it easy, so nobody stays home because they “can’t cook”. And we keep it friendly, so nobody feels they have to prove anything.

why the group matters

One person with a recipe makes a snack. Fifteen make an evening.

The dessert is never the hard part. The good part is what happens around the table while it’s being made.

Everyone has a job

Someone crushes, someone melts, someone layers, someone does the tasting. Nobody stands around watching, so nobody stands around alone.

Nobody is a guest

There’s no chef and no audience. We made it together, so it belongs to everyone at the table, including the person who arrived ten minutes ago.

It keeps going

People come back, bring a flatmate, offer their kitchen table for the next one. That’s how a one-off evening turns into a group.

the name

Why “cooks without fires”?

Because nobody involved had a real kitchen. Student rooms and shared flats come with two hobs that half work, no oven, and a fridge shared between five people.

That could have been a reason not to do it. Instead it became the rule that makes it work: if the recipe needs no fire, everybody can take part, including the person who has never made anything in their life.

It also decides what we make. Desserts and cakes are where cooking without heat stops being a compromise: things that get crushed, whipped, layered and left in the fridge.

The barrier to joining is turning up. That’s it.

to be clear

What we are, and what we aren’t

  • A group of people. Students, researchers, workers, newcomers and locals.
  • Free and open. Anyone can come, especially if you don’t know anyone yet.
  • A place to laugh. At the conversation, and at the occasional collapsed cheesecake.
  • Growing, one city at a time. Padova first, maybe yours next.
  • Not a competition. No jury, no ranking, no “best dessert”. We all eat the same cake.
  • Not a cooking class. Nobody is paid and nobody pays. Nobody grades your whisking.
  • Not a potluck. Please don’t bring a dish. Everything is already there.
  • Not a dating app or a networking event. People do help each other with flats and jobs, but the way neighbours do, not because it’s the agenda.

who runs it

All of us, a bit at a time

A small rotating group of people who have been coming since the start. Whoever has the space that month hosts and does the shopping; everybody else turns up. There’s no committee, and nothing to join beyond arriving.

Have a big table and a fridge with room in it? Say so: that’s how the next sessions get scheduled. Live somewhere else? Start one in your city.
