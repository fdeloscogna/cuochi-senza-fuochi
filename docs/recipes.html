The recipes

No oven · no hob · no skill

Everything here can be made in a bedroom

These are the recipes that get made together at a session, and they are all chosen for the same reason: a bowl, a knife and a fridge is the entire equipment list. Take any of them home and you can do it again on your own.

Come and make one

The house speciality

Desserts, because heat is the thing you don’t have

Dessert is where cooking without fire stops being a limitation and starts being an advantage. Almost nothing good in this category was ever baked.

Salame di cioccolato

The one that comes up most. Crush dry biscuits in a bag, melt dark chocolate with butter, mix with a little sugar, roll it into a log in baking paper and put it in the fridge for two hours. It slices like a salami and it never fails.

Tiramisù

No bake by definition. Mascarpone, eggs, sugar, coffee, savoiardi, cocoa. The only hard part is not moving the tray too fast on the way to the fridge.

No bake cheesecake

A crushed biscuit and butter base pressed into a tin, cream cheese whipped with sugar and lemon on top, then several hours of doing nothing at all. Looks like the hardest thing on this page and is the easiest.

Fruit with lemon and sugar

Chop whatever is in season, add sugar and lemon juice, thirty minutes in the fridge and it makes its own syrup. Costs almost nothing and everybody takes some.

Chocolate mousse, two ingredients

Dark chocolate and cream, whipped. That is the whole recipe. Good in small glasses, and it makes the room smell like a much more serious kitchen than it is.

Affogato

Very cold vanilla ice cream, very hot espresso poured over it at the table. Not really a recipe, more a reliable way to end an evening.

Savoury, for before the sugar

Same rule applies. Nothing here goes near a flame.

Hummus, the lazy version

Blitz a tin of chickpeas with tahini, lemon, garlic and plenty of olive oil. No blender in the flat? Mash it with a fork and call it rustic. Bring carrots and bread.

Panzanella

Tear up yesterday’s bread, add chopped tomatoes, red onion, basil, a lot of olive oil, a splash of vinegar and salt. Twenty minutes of sitting still and the bread softens.

Carrot, orange and cumin

Grate carrots, squeeze in an orange, add a big pinch of cumin, oil and salt. Three ingredients, five minutes, and it looks like somebody tried.

A proper board

Cheese, olives, fruit, bread, nuts. Zero cooking, arranged on anything flat. Always one of the first things to disappear.

Why these and not others

  • Nothing needs heat. No oven, no hob, no risk of a shared kitchen being occupied.
  • Everything survives being made by fifteen people at once. Recipes that need precision or timing do not work in a room full of people talking.
  • The steps split up. Somebody crushes, somebody melts, somebody rolls. If a recipe can only be done by one pair of hands, it is the wrong recipe for a group.
  • You can repeat it alone. Every one of these is doable in a bedroom with a fridge down the hall, which is the whole point of the club.

Pick one and come and make it

You don’t need to buy anything or practise first. Just turn up.
