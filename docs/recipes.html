Recipes

No oven · no skill · under €5

What to bring when you can’t cook

Every dish here feeds 4–6 people, costs a few euro, and needs nothing more than a bowl, a knife and a fridge. This is the senza fuochi part of the name.

Start here

The absolute minimum

If you’re reading this an hour before the dinner: buy good bread, a jar of olives, and a bar of dark chocolate. Total cost about €6. You have contributed. Come anyway.

Nothing to cook at all

🍅

Panzanella

Tear up yesterday’s bread, add chopped tomatoes, red onion, basil, plenty of olive oil, a splash of vinegar and salt. Leave it 20 minutes so the bread softens. Better the longer it sits, which makes it perfect for carrying across town.

🥕

Carrot, orange and cumin salad

Grate carrots, squeeze in an orange, add a big pinch of cumin, oil, salt. Three ingredients, five minutes, and it looks like you tried.

🫘

Chickpea and tuna salad

Two tins of chickpeas, one of tuna, half a red onion sliced thin, lemon, oil, parsley. High protein, costs about €3, and it survives a bike ride.

🥣

Hummus, the lazy version

Blitz a tin of chickpeas with tahini, lemon, garlic and a lot of olive oil. No blender? Mash it with a fork and call it rustic. Bring carrots and bread to dip.

🧀

A proper board

Cheese, cured meat or olives, fruit, bread, nuts. Zero cooking. Arrange it on anything flat. This is always one of the first things to disappear.

🍉

Watermelon, feta and mint

Cube the watermelon, crumble feta over it, tear in mint, black pepper. Summer only, gone in four minutes.

One hob, one pan

🍝

Pasta al pesto, cold

Boil pasta, drain, rinse under cold water so it doesn’t clump, stir through pesto with a spoonful of the cooking water. Add cherry tomatoes and pine nuts if the budget stretches. Travels fine, eats fine at room temperature.

🍚

Rice salad

Boil rice, cool it, then throw in whatever is cheap: tinned corn, olives, tuna, diced cheese, pickles. Dress with oil and lemon. Infinitely forgiving.

🥔

Frittata di patate

Boil potatoes, slice them, fry with onion, pour over six beaten eggs, cook slowly, flip with the help of a plate and some courage. Good hot, good cold, feeds many.

🍲

Lentil soup

Onion, carrot, celery, a tin of tomatoes, a bag of red lentils, water, salt. Twenty-five minutes unattended. Cheapest way to feed eight people.

Dessert without an oven

🍫

Salame di cioccolato

Crush dry biscuits, melt dark chocolate and butter, mix with a little sugar, roll into a log in baking paper, fridge for two hours, slice. No oven, and it looks impressive on the table.

☕

Tiramisù, no-bake by definition

Mascarpone, eggs, sugar, coffee, savoiardi, cocoa. It was never baked to begin with. Make it the night before and don’t move it too fast.

🍓

Fruit with lemon and sugar

Chop whatever fruit is in season, sugar, lemon juice, thirty minutes in the fridge. It becomes its own syrup. Costs nothing and everyone takes some.

Rules of thumb for potluck cooking

  • Room temperature is your friend. Nothing that has to be served piping hot.
  • Cook for 4–6, not for everyone. With fifteen dishes on the table, portions are small.
  • Bring it in the container you’ll carry home. Nobody wants to redistribute your dish at 1am.
  • Label it if it matters. Contains nuts, contains pork, vegan — a scrap of paper is enough.
  • One dish is one dish. You are not hosting. You are contributing.
