what it takes

Less than you’d think

No venue to rent, no equipment to buy, no budget to raise. Just these four things.

A table and a fridge

A shared flat, a cohousing common room, a student residence, a friend’s kitchen. Anywhere people can stand around a table.

Cheap ingredients

Biscuits, chocolate, cream, fruit. Our recipes are picked to feed a crowd from a single supermarket run.

Ten-ish curious people

Flatmates, classmates, colleagues, a post in a local group. Strangers are welcome; that’s the point.

One person who says “let’s”

Someone to pick a date and send the first message. That could be you.

starting one

How we’d start it, together

  • Write to us

    A message on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. Tell us your city and who you think might come.

  • We share what we know

    The recipes that work with a crowd, what to buy, and how we run an evening from the first hello to the last spoon.

  • Host your first one

    Pick an easy dessert, invite a few people, and let everyone make it together. Keep it small the first time.

  • Send us the photos

    Tell us how it went. Then do it again next month, and watch the group grow.

same spirit everywhere

The few things every city keeps

Every city will have its own recipes and its own crowd. These four things stay the same, because they’re what make it work.

  • Free to join. Nobody pays at the door. Ingredients stay cheap so it stays that way.
  • No competition. We make one dessert together, as a team. Nobody wins, everybody eats.
  • No fire needed. No oven, no stove, so any room works and anyone can take part.
  • Everybody’s welcome. Especially the person who’s new and came on their own.
