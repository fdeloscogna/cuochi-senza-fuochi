Cuochi senza fuochi (it means cooks without fires) is a group of people who get together to make easy, cheap desserts and cakes, with no oven and no stove. The cake is the excuse. The people you make it with are the point.

who we are

A group of people, not a cooking school.

Moving to a new city, you sort out a room and a commute in a few weeks. Finding people takes longer. So we keep it simple: one table, one easy recipe, a bowl each, and whoever turns up. While your hands are busy with biscuits and cream, talking to strangers just happens.

Together, never against

No jury, no score, no “best cake”. We all make the same dessert together, and we all eat it. The only thing that gets judged is how fast the tiramisù disappears.

Cheap on purpose

Joining is free. Recipes are made from a few supermarket basics, so money is never the reason someone stays home.

Easy for everyone

No oven, no stove, no experience. If you can stir, crush a biscuit or lick a spoon, you are already qualified.

Nobody wins. Everybody eats.

the whole idea, in four words

an evening with us

Four steps, and the first one takes a minute

No form, no fee, nothing to prepare. This is the whole format.

  • Send us a message

    One message on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube. We reply with the place, the time and the dessert of the evening.

  • Come empty-handed

    Don’t cook and don’t bring a dish. The ingredients and the bowls are already on the table when you arrive.

  • Make it together

    Someone talks everyone through the recipe. You crush, mix and layer, chatting with whoever is standing next to you.

  • Eat it, keep the people

    Into the fridge, onto the plates, gone. You go home with a recipe you can redo without a kitchen, and a few new names in your phone.

why come along

We’re not the best cooks in town. We’re good company.

There are lots of good ways to meet people. This one is just very easy to say yes to, whoever you are and however long you’ve been here.

  • New in town
  • Erasmus and exchange
  • PhD students and researchers
  • Just started a job here
  • Living in a cohousing or a shared flat
  • Here for years, up for new faces
  • You don’t need to know anyone. Most people come on their own. It’s the normal way to arrive, and nobody stays a stranger for long.
  • You don’t need to cook. Every recipe is chosen so a total beginner gets it right the first time.
  • You don’t need to spend. Joining is free, and nobody collects money at the door.
  • You leave with more than dessert. A recipe you can repeat with no kitchen, and people to share the next one with.
